# Privacy Policy for ዘና BINGO Cards

**Last Updated: August 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **ዘና BINGO Cards** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **Birehanu Kassa**, handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**ዘና BINGO Cards** is designed as a standalone digital Bingo card player.
- **No Personal Data Collected**: The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, contacts, or financial details).
- **Local Storage Only**: Any app preferences (such as selected theme, sound settings, or active card numbers) are stored locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The App requests only standard, non-sensitive permissions:
- **INTERNET Access**: Used solely to load local web assets, render hybrid interface components within Capacitor, and enable standard web-view capabilities. No personal data is tracked or transmitted.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does **not** integrate third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or data-broker services.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is safe for all audiences.

---

### 5. Security
We value your trust. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, your private information remains secure on your own device.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
